Dating that belongs to your community.

NexaHeart is a free, open-source dating app for iPhone, Android and the web. A city, a university or any community can run it under its own name, on its own server. No ads, no paywalls, no selling data.

Run it for your community See the app

The app speaks English, Hebrew, Arabic and Russian, right to left included.

Built for fewer, better conversations.

Most dating apps earn more the longer you keep swiping. NexaHeart earns nothing, so it can be built for the opposite: people who like each other, real conversations, and a kind goodbye when it isn't a match.

Every feature is free for everyone, including seeing who liked you. The code is public under the MIT license, so anyone can check what it does with your data.

Matching

Matches that explain themselves

NexaHeart suggests people who are likely to like each other, and says why: a shared language, the same idea of a perfect Sunday. Attention is spread fairly, so nobody gets buried. The algorithm is published.

  • Daily picks, chosen with care
  • Like a specific answer and add a comment
  • "Likes You" is free
Safety

Kindness is built into the chat

Before a hurtful message goes out, NexaHeart asks "Are you sure?". Offensive messages are hidden, common scam patterns get a warning, and photos stay blurred until you choose to look. A report button is always one tap away.

New in 0.2

Safer dates, and no ghosting

Going on a date? Set a safety check-in. Someone you trust gets an email only if you don't tap "I'm OK" in time, or right away if you press SOS. When a chat isn't going anywhere, "Close kindly" ends it with a polite message instead of silence.

  • The email holds only what you wrote, in your language
  • Check-ins are deleted 7 days after they end
  • Chats waiting 3 days for your reply are marked
New

See each other before you meet

Matches can have a video date or a voice call right in the app, without sharing phone numbers. Calls run on the community's own server and are never recorded. The other person always chooses whether to join, and everyone joins with the camera off.

  • Only after you both wrote a few messages
  • Report or block from the call: it ends at once
  • Off until the community's admins turn it on
Privacy

Privacy you can check

Consent comes first, and sensitive details need their own explicit yes. Your location is shown only as an area of about 1 km. "Download my data" and "Delete my account" are two taps away, and there are no tracking cookies at all.

AI

AI that runs on your server

Matching, conversation starters and a gentle profile coach run on the community's own server by default. A cloud AI service is used only if the operator turns it on and each member agrees.

For the people who run it

Your name, your server, your rules

One install script gives your community its own app, with your name, logo and colours. The data stays on your server.

Moderation included

A dashboard with mandatory two-factor sign-in, a report queue with evidence, photo verification reviewed by a person, and a full audit log.

Runs on one server

PostgreSQL, Redis and S3-compatible storage in Docker, with HTTPS, virus scanning for uploads and backups. Load-tested with 5,000 members.

No vendor in the middle

No analytics or third-party scripts. Email goes through the provider you choose, and push notifications are opt-in and carry no names or message text.

Start in an afternoon

You need a Linux server with Docker and a domain name. The installer asks a few questions and starts everything.

  • HTTPS certificates set up for you
  • A setup page to claim the instance and pick your brand
  • A production checklist before you invite members

Questions and answers

How much does it cost?

The software is free and open source, with no paid tier. You pay only for your server, your domain and an email sending service, at the prices of the providers you choose.

What do I need to know to run it?

How to connect to a server over SSH, paste commands and edit a text file. The guide checks each step with you. For a growing community, someone comfortable with Linux should look after updates and backups.

Where is our members' data stored?

Only on your server. Nothing goes to the NexaHeart authors. Emails go through your email provider, and optional push notifications through Expo, Apple and Google, without names or message text.

Which languages does the app speak?

English, Hebrew, Arabic and Russian, right to left for Hebrew and Arabic. Each member picks a language; you choose the default.

Is AI required?

No. By default there is no AI model at all. The optional local AI runs on your own server, and a cloud AI service is used only if you set one up and each member agrees.

Is it legal to run a dating app for our community?

It depends on your country and audience, so ask a lawyer. The repository includes draft legal texts and a privacy guide listing every kind of data, as a starting point, not as legal advice.
