## Who else sees your data

- **Other members** see your profile (never your email, exact location or birth date) and the
  messages you send them. In a call, the other person sees and hears only what you choose to share (your camera is off
  until you turn it on); the app never records calls, but the other person could record their screen ⚖️. Calls are
  end-to-end encrypted. Captions, if the other person turns them on, are made on your own device and
  are never stored.
- **Our moderators** see reports, the reported content and verification selfies, and only for that
  purpose.
- **Service providers** that process data for us under contract: [HOSTING_PROVIDER] ([COUNTRY]),
  [EMAIL_PROVIDER] (email). If you turn on notifications: Expo, Apple or Google deliver them, with no
  names or message text. [If enabled: external AI provider [AI_PROVIDER], only if you turn on "AI
  features that use an external provider"; it receives your bio and prompt text only.]
- **Partner communities**, only if you join them in the app: other NexaHeart communities this one
  connected with, each run by its own operator under an agreement with us ⚖️. They receive your card
  (first name, age, gender, what you're looking for, bio, prompts, photos, languages, verified) under
  a code name, and, when you search, your gender, who you're interested in and your age range. Your
  likes go to them, and a match, and the messages you exchange in it, are kept by both until either of you ends it. Never your email, location, religion,
  accessibility needs, voice or messages. Leaving tells them to forget you. If you report someone from a partner community, your report and the latest messages of your chat go to that community's moderators.
- **Authorities**, only when the law requires it.

We do not sell or rent personal data and do not use it for advertising.

## Automated decisions

Matching ranks people by distance, shared preferences and similar interests, and shows you the
reasons. It does not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you. Automatic
filters can hide offensive messages or flag possible scams; a person reviews every report before
action is taken against an account.

## Your rights

You can: access and download your data (Settings → Download my data); correct it (your profile);
delete your account and data (Settings → Delete account); withdraw consents (Settings); object to
processing or ask us to restrict it; and complain to a supervisory authority (in Israel: the Privacy
Protection Authority; in the EU: the authority in your country). Write to [CONTACT_EMAIL]; we answer
within [30 days / one month].

## Security

Encrypted connections (HTTPS), photos re-encoded with their metadata removed, uploads scanned for
malware, staff accounts protected with two-factor authentication, an audit trail of staff actions.
No system is perfectly secure; we will tell you if a breach puts you at high risk.
