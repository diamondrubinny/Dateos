# Video ad

`nexaheart-ad-he.mp4` and `nexaheart-ad-en.mp4`: 32 s, vertical 1080×1920 (Reels, TikTok, Shorts,
Stories), with real app screenshots from `docs/screenshots/` and an original, generated soundtrack.

To render again: put the Heebo fonts (`@fontsource/heebo`, OFL) and `apps/mobile/assets/icon.png`
next to `source/ad.html`, link `shots/he` and `shots/en` to `docs/screenshots/<lang>`, then
`node render.mjs he out.mp4` (needs Playwright and ffmpeg) and add a soundtrack with ffmpeg.

⚖️ Check the claims against each community's terms and privacy policy before publishing.
